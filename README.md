# BOSS – Business Operating System Solutions

## Project Overview

BOSS (Business Operating System Solutions) is a web application designed to bring the everyday functions needed to run a business into one centralized system.

The goal of BOSS is to create a flexible business management system that can be used by many different types of businesses instead of being designed for one specific industry.

A business owner should be able to open BOSS and quickly see what needs to be done, what is scheduled, what money is coming in or going out, and what areas of the business need attention.

## Project Purpose

My idea for BOSS comes from my experience working for myself and managing different types of work. Running a business involves much more than completing the actual work.

Schedules, tasks, contacts, expenses, payments, employees, documents, and other responsibilities all need to be managed on a daily basis.

BOSS is intended to organize these functions into one easy-to-use web application.

## General Functionality

- Dashboard – Overview of important business activity, priorities, upcoming items, and financial information.
- Command Center – Daily tasks, schedules, deadlines, reminders, and items requiring attention.
- Work & Connections – Business activity, customers, suppliers, vendors, and other contacts.
- Money & Sales – Income, expenses, invoices, payments, and sales activity.
- Team & Resources – Employees, responsibilities, schedules, documents, and business resources.
- Control Center – Company information, users, permissions, and application settings.
- Search & Quick Actions – Fast access to information and commonly used actions.

## Project Goal

The overall goal is to make BOSS a central operating system for the day-to-day management of a business while keeping the system flexible enough to work for businesses in different industries.
